Who's drawing

I'm Maruf, MD. Mochaddec Hossain on paper.

By day I'm a Technical Product Designer at LifeArk Co. Ltd. I studied Computer Science at AIUB. In AIUB Arts Club I ran sketch workshops, organized events, and contributed as an artist.

After hours it's a pencil, a sketchbook and a face I'm trying to get right, one layer of graphite at a time. I sign the pages Maruf Talha and keep the sketchbook open at @talhas_sketchbook.
